PRED-002 AUG 2026 MEDIUM CONFIDENCEPREDICTION

World systems will play a larger role than in GTA V

Rockstar appears to be emphasizing environmental interaction and systemic world behavior — NPC routines, wildlife, weather, and the rebuilt witness-driven wanted system all point the same way.

This remains a prediction rather than a confirmed feature, but the witness/alarm wanted rebuild and individually art-directed NPCs strengthen it.

PRED-003 AUG 2026 LOW CONFIDENCESPECULATION

Additional major regions may become accessible

Geographic hints make additional explorable areas plausible, although the evidence remains limited — and the confirmed scale figures argue the launch map is already enormous without them.

This entry should be treated as speculation. Most plausible as post-launch additions, if at all.

PRED-004 SEP 2026 HIGH CONFIDENCEPREDICTION

PC release follows console launch on Rockstar's pattern

No PC version is confirmed — but every major Rockstar release since GTA IV has shipped console-first and PC 12–24 months later. Expect the same cadence, not an exception.

SOURCE: Rockstar release history (GTA IV, GTA V, RDR2). OBSERVATION: no PC SKU announced alongside PS5/Xbox Series X|S. CONTEXT: double-dip economics plus PC-specific optimization work. INFERENCE: PC in 2027–2028. High confidence in the pattern, not a date.

PRED-005 SEP 2026 MEDIUM CONFIDENCEPREDICTION

Multiplayer / GTA VI Online lands in 2027

Reported but unconfirmed: a multiplayer component follows the single-player launch, mirroring GTA Online's post-launch arrival. Treat timing as inference, not fact.

SOURCE: secondary reporting plus Rockstar history. OBSERVATION: no official multiplayer details or date. CONTEXT: GTA Online launched two weeks after GTA V; RDR Online followed RDR2. INFERENCE: a 2027 multiplayer launch window is the base case.

PRED-006 SEP 2026 MEDIUM CONFIDENCEPREDICTION

No 60 FPS performance mode at launch — later patch likely

Previews confirm a 30 FPS target. Given PS5 Pro and Rockstar's patch history, a performance mode may arrive post-launch — but buying the game on that hope is speculative.

SOURCE: preview coverage (CNF-011) plus console hardware reporting. OBSERVATION: 30 FPS lock confirmed; no performance mode announced. CONTEXT: GTA V / RDR2 both gained enhanced modes later. INFERENCE: 60 FPS comes, if at all, after launch.

PRED-007 SEP 2026 LOW CONFIDENCESPECULATION

Launch trailer lands in late October / early November

Rockstar's marketing cadence — trailer beats tightening toward release — suggests one final launch trailer roughly 2–4 weeks before 19 November. Timing guesses are low-confidence by nature.

SOURCE: Rockstar marketing history (GTA V, RDR2 trailer cadence). OBSERVATION: Extended Look aired 27 August; preload begins 12 November. CONTEXT: a launch-trailer slot between mid-October and early November fits the gap. INFERENCE: watch that window. Low confidence.
