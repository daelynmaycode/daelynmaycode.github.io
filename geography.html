THE CENTRAL FINDING ROCKSTAR HAS OFFICIALLY NAMED SEVEN PLACES.

Not seventy. Seven. They are the titles of Rockstar's own place artwork, the six destinations linked from its "Only in Leonida" page, and one facility named in official character copy. A much larger list of counties, districts, roads, an airport and a raceway circulates online and is not established — the community source carrying it banners itself as possibly leaked. Those names are quarantined in Unidentified / Uncertain and are never mapped, counted, or used to infer structure.

NATURAL ENVIRONMENTS

Ecosystems — and the ones that are missing

The official record establishes a coastal urban zone, an island chain and one protected upland area. It establishes no wetland, no farmland and no forest. Those absences are recorded here as findings rather than left as silence.

WATER GEOGRAPHY

Ocean, shore and what is unnamed

Coast and boat yards are established. No river, lake, bay or named water body appears anywhere in the official record — which matters, because a coastal state with an uncharacterised interior is a very different proposition from one with mapped hydrology.

TRANSPORTATION

The largest gap in the record

Rockstar has named no highway, road, bridge, causeway, railway, port, marina or airport anywhere in Leonida. Air and sea freight are established only through smuggling references. That is not a formatting gap — it is the single largest hole in the official geographic record, and it is documented as such.

WILDLIFE & ECOLOGY

Two mascots and a flock of birds

Official material names exactly one observed animal group — birds flying "in perfect formation" — and two cultural mascots, an alligator and a manatee. Mascot status is a branding fact, not an ecological one, and this section keeps the two apart deliberately.

WEATHER & CLIMATE

Sunlight, and almost nothing else

"The sunniest place in America" is the most repeated piece of environmental world-building Rockstar has published, appearing across the site, the places page and official merchandise. No temperature, rainfall, storm or fog data exists. This is deliberately not a climate section, because there is no climate data to summarise.

ARCHITECTURE

Built environment

Only Vice City has any architectural description, and even there the record is a mood — neon, high-rises, a retro pastiche — rather than a building type. Where GTA VI resembles real Miami, that resemblance is marked as observable. No architectural inspiration is ever asserted, because Rockstar has never stated one.

ECONOMIC GEOGRAPHY

What people are officially shown doing

Geography to economy to infrastructure, read strictly from official copy. No economic statistic is inferred from visual impression, and none is published by Rockstar. The state-wide pattern is legible even so: tourism everywhere, night-time commerce in the city, recorded music bridging the towns, and contraband running out of the Keys.

SOCIAL & CULTURAL GEOGRAPHY

Tourism culture, television and the Keys

Leonida's official cultural identity is a tourism identity, and Rockstar has named exactly one piece of Leonida popular culture: the state TV show Macca the Gator. Claims about who lives where, or what any region's sub-culture is, are not established and are not made.

ENVIRONMENTAL TRANSITIONS

Where one place becomes another

Transitions reveal how a world is constructed, but Rockstar has published no boundary distances and no route descriptions. Every distance field on this page therefore reads UNKNOWN. That the transitions exist is a reasonable inference from the place list; how far they take to cross is simply not published.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT

Fictional history and the continuity question

Leonida has a smuggling golden age, a state apparatus and a hit TV show, but no dates. On continuity: GTA VI's relationship to Vice City (2002) is a documented look, sold as the Vintage Vice City Pack's flashback to "when the neon burned brightest". That is reinterpreted presentation, not confirmed narrative continuity — the older game's plot, cast and map are not imported.

REAL-WORLD PARALLELS

Leonida and Florida — kept apart

Real-world similarity is contextual evidence, not proof of fictional geography. It is useful for explaining why Leonida reads the way it does, and for nothing else. The most important row below is the one that refuses an inference: real Florida's Everglades is a wetland, and GTA VI has established no wetland at all.

UNIDENTIFIED / UNCERTAIN

Names that circulate but are not established

These names appear in community maps, wikis and video essays. None is attributable to Rockstar, and the principal source that compiles them states on its own page that it may contain leaked content. They are listed here so a visitor who meets them elsewhere can recognise them for what they are.

HANDLING NOT PLOTTED. NOT COUNTED. NOT INFERRED FROM.

GLOSSARY

The distinctions this page protects

Most geographic errors about Leonida come from collapsing these terms. In particular: absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, and a destination is not the same thing as a place.
