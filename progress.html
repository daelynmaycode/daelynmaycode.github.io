How the gates work. Each track lists four gates — system, geometry, art and polish. A gate is filled only when the corresponding work is signed off in the build. No percentage on this page is an estimate of remaining time.
01

World & environments

In development

A continuous 16 km² overworld streamed in 128 m cells, with day/night, seven weather states and a shared ocean.

Systems Geometry Art Polish
Working 2,000 × 2,000 terrain cells at 2 m; 4,000 m world; 81 chunks resident at the south gate; asynchronous cell streaming with distance LODs.
Art in Triplanar PBR terrain for grass, forest floor, rock, sand and snow; five authored tree species with near meshes and billboard HLODs.
Missing Authored coast mesh with matching collision and cliff transitions, final water and rock materials, grass alpha cards and the final waterfall art.
02

Towns, settlements & dungeons

In development

Eight settlement anchors from the approved layout. Coneria is rebuilt from its own NES town plan; the rest are scaled blockouts.

Systems Geometry Art Polish
Coneria 214 × 163 m stepped perimeter traced from the town map at 0.21 m per plan pixel; two axial gates, crossroad square, four canal reaches with stone bridges, seven buildings including the inn.
Interiors Seven enterable doors and eight interior spaces reported by the boot smoke test; castle first floor dressed with runner, carpets and railings. A November asset pass dressed them with scanned books, wine bottles, cargo crates, shovels, shop shelves and inn beds.
Missing Detailed districts for the other seven towns, all dungeon interiors, and the castle room partition layout (the reference plan was not available).
03

Characters & NPCs

In development

Four rigged hero classes driven by a data file, with exported MetaHuman bodies in the intake slot, and residents and animals with contextual behaviour.

Systems Geometry Art Polish
Working heroes.json defines identity, height, palette and proportions for Fighter (male and female), Thief and Monk over a shared 92-bone Humanoid contract. Four exported MetaHuman bodies now sit at the declared paths under assets/characters/heroes/.
Pipeline MetaHuman bone renaming, height normalisation and proportional stylisation; a Ghibli-style shading pass restyles both MetaHuman and authored bodies into one visual language.
Gaps The MetaHuman body source is declared and exported, but the newest hero capture still renders the authored fallback, so the switch is not yet proven in a frame. That fallback's supplied modular head reads as a doll at conversation distance. Villagers are procedural; remaining classes are unmodelled.
04

Combat & gameplay systems

In development

Real-time field combat works. The turn-based battle layer that will resolve FF1 encounters is specified and staged, with no runtime yet.

Systems Geometry Art Polish
Working Basic attack, lock-on and a target acquisition/retention system verified at 67 checks; survival model with health, fatigue, hydration and stamina; swimming, diving and mounted travel.
Rules A read-only typed layer loads all 128 enemies, 44 AI records, 128 formations and 128 domains once at startup and refuses to run on tainted tables.
Not built Active Time Battle, the battle arena, damage and status resolution, and the field-to-battle bridge. Encounter selection and count rolls are deliberately withheld until the resolution core exists.
05

Story, quests & progression

In development

The journal, tracker and opening cinematic are built. Authored story content currently stops at Coneria.

Systems Content Polish
Working Tales of Light: main story, side quests, completed tales and discoveries, priority tale, stage tracking and a compact objective tracker. A skippable opening cinematic sets the prophecy.
Backed by A single RPG state autoload owns quest stages, priorities and discoveries, emits a change signal and saves with the journey. The Field Menu never keeps a second copy.
Not built Experience, levels, spell charges, loot tables and persistence of progression — the whole S4 stage. Story content beyond the capital is unwritten.
06

Weapons, armor & equipment

In development

The equipment system and its interface are working. The original item, weapon, armor and shop tables have not been extracted yet.

Systems Content Art
Working Five-slot contract — main hand, off hand, head, body, accessory — with a visible loadout on the character, an equipment comparison view, starter gear, a satchel and key items.
Restrictions Class restrictions and per-slot defaults are normalised on save so a malformed loadout cannot persist.
Missing ROM weapon, armor, item and shop tables (M2), plus weapon and armor models for the gallery's equipment set.
07

Graphics, lighting & rendering

In development

A modern Forward+ pipeline with global illumination approximations, quality tiers and capability gating — running on blockout geometry.

Systems Art Optimise
High preset SDFGI, SSIL, SSAO, screen-space reflections, volumetric fog, cascaded soft shadows, Filmic tonemapping, auto exposure and TAA.
Measured 57.4 FPS average in the town with 2,563 draw calls and 1,823 MB VRAM at the last audit. These figures predate the most recent rendering work and have not been re-measured.
Note These are Nanite- and Lumen-inspired approximations on Godot's native renderer — not virtual geometry or software ray tracing.
08

Audio & soundtrack

In development

Routing, mixing and soundtrack selection are built. The audio itself is reference material that must be replaced before release.

Systems Content Mix
Working Seven routed and independently mixable buses; selectable NES and Pixel Remaster soundtracks resolved centrally and crossfaded across overworld, town, battle, castle and menu sequences.
Persisted Bus levels and soundtrack choice survive a restart; a separate-process check reloads and re-applies every audio, graphics and input value.
Legal Both soundtracks are user-supplied reference audio. They are study material only and cannot ship.
09

AI & character behaviour

In development

Field behaviour — aggro, animal temperament, resident social state — runs. Battle AI exists as decoded tables with no executor.

Field AI Data Battle AI
Field Enemy spawn authority and manager, aggro and movement profiles; animals with temperament, trust, mood and a behaviour state machine plus synthesised vocalisations.
Social Residents keep hostility, warning and alert state, so the same verb reads differently on a calm, irritated or hostile villager; a line composer reacts to role, weather, threat and recent dialogue.
Battle All 44 enemy AI records are decoded and cross-validated — spell slots, special slots and chance weights — but nothing consumes them yet.
10

UI/UX & menus

Implemented

The most complete part of the build: a full Field Menu, pause menu, Settings suite, HUD and accessibility layer, all persistent.

Systems Layout Verified Art
Menus Field Menu with eight sections — overview, status, equipment, items, magic, formation, tales and discoveries. Pause menu limited to exactly five choices.
Settings Graphics, Input and Sound categories: resolution and quality tiers, effect toggles, full keyboard and controller remapping, deadzone and sensitivity, accessibility options.
Verified Layouts checked at 1024 × 768, 1280 × 720, 1600 × 900 and 1920 × 800; settings persistence verified across two separate processes.
One known failure. The controls suite reports 323 passes and a single failure: "jump acts as swim-up while swimming". It is in the underwater input path, far from the town work that produced it, and is recorded rather than hidden. Every other suite exits clean.
