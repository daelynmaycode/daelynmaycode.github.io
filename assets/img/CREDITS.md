# Image provenance

Every image on this site is a real capture from the FF1 3D Remake project
(`FF1-3DRemake/verification/` on the development machine). None were made for the
website, and none are concept art presented as gameplay.

Files were downscaled to a maximum width of 1280 px (1100 px for character and
enemy portraits) and re-encoded as JPEG at quality 82. The three NES images are
unmodified 256×224 PNG captures.
