Weapons & Combat

An evidence-based log of GTA VI's combat, pursuit, and evasion systems — tracked separately from the main predictions index.

EVIDENCE STANDARD PUBLIC MATERIAL ONLY.

Entries derive from official footage and attributable reporting. Back to the main index: ← PREDICTIONS

WPN-001 SEP 2026 COMBATOBSERVED

Gunplay rebuilt around weight and consequence

Shown combat emphasizes heavier weapon handling, reactive hit response, and situational awareness over the snappy arcade gunplay of GTA V — closer in spirit to RDR2's deliberate shootouts, tuned for an urban arsenal.

SOURCE: official trailers and Extended Look combat sequences. OBSERVATION: visible recoil discipline, cover-to-cover movement, varied enemy hit reactions. CONTEXT: RDR2 overhauled Rockstar gunfeel (locational damage, weapon maintenance); GTA VI inherits that trajectory, not GTA V's. INFERENCE: combat is a systemic pressure valve — loud, costly, consequential — feeding the witness/evasion loop.

WPN-002 SEP 2026 DE-ESCALATIONINFERRED

Aiming without firing can de-escalate

Brandishing a weapon without shooting is reportedly a distinct interaction state: civilians comply, clerks cooperate, and some encounters resolve without a shot — intimidation as a mechanic, not just a prelude.

SOURCE: preview reporting on robbery and intimidation interactions. OBSERVATION: aim-at-NPC behavior producing compliance rather than instant flight. CONTEXT: RDR2's hold-to-aim greet/antagonize ladder is the direct ancestor. INFERENCE: a full intimidation ladder (aim → warn → fire) that lets players calibrate heat.

WPN-003 SEP 2026 EVASIONINFERRED

Parkour escapes matter at 3★ and above

Rooftop routes, climbs, vaults, and alley chains are shown as viable pursuit-breakers once heat passes a threshold — foot pursuits become a traversal puzzle, and the 600k-animation budget suddenly makes sense.

SOURCE: official footage of rooftop/foot pursuits plus preview reporting. OBSERVATION: climb, vault, and slide traversal mid-pursuit. CONTEXT: classic GTA foot evasion was sprint + corner-break; dense vertical Vice City rewards a traversal layer. INFERENCE: stamina + traversal skill gates high- star foot escapes; map design assumes it.

WPN-004 SEP 2026 STEALTHINFERRED

Masks, descriptions, and the identity economy

Face coverings, outfit swaps, and vehicle changes plug directly into the witness system: police circulate a description, and altering what you look like — or what you drive — degrades it. Trunk wardrobes make the getaway car a changing room.

SOURCE: preview reporting on masks, descriptions, and trunk-stored outfits (see CNF-010). OBSERVATION: identity vectors (face/clothes/ vehicle) with wardrobe counters. CONTEXT: heist masks were cosmetic in GTA V; here disguise is load-bearing. INFERENCE: pre-crime preparation (mask on, clean car staged) becomes core loop — stealth through identity management.
