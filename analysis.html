ANL-001 SEP 2026 TECHNICALINFERRED

Map scale figures as streaming-budget evidence

A map ~2× GTA V (3× RDR2), with Vice City alone at ~2× Los Santos, is not just a geography claim — it is a claim about streaming throughput, memory budgets, and level-of-detail systems on fixed console hardware.

SOURCE: attributed Rockstar North statement (Rob Nelson). OBSERVATION: map ≈ 2× GTA V / 3× RDR2; Vice City ≈ 2× Los Santos. CONTEXT: open-world streaming scales with density × traversal speed; SSDs remove seek limits, not memory limits. INFERENCE: Rockstar rebuilt its streaming/LOD pipeline — tiered detail, fast paging — to hold this scale at 30 FPS.

ANL-002 SEP 2026 TECHNICALINFERRED

600,000 animations: what the figure implies

600,000+ animations — against ~55k in GTA V and ~300k in RDR2 — suggests a shift from hand-authored clips toward layered, procedural, and context-blended animation systems, especially for NPCs.

SOURCE: attributed Rockstar reporting on animation counts; NPC art-direction attributed to Aaron Garbut. OBSERVATION: 600k+ vs ~55k (GTA V) vs ~300k (RDR2). CONTEXT: no studio hand-authors 600k unique clips — counts at this scale imply parametric variation, mirroring, retargeting, and procedural layers. INFERENCE: the budget funds NPC variety and systemic behavior more than protagonist moves — crowd believability is the target.

ANL-003 SEP 2026 TECHNICALINFERRED

The witness-driven wanted system as a simulation problem

Police that track face, clothes, and vehicle — with hollow stars, outfit changes, and trunk wardrobes as counters — turn evasion from a radius timer into an information-state problem: what do witnesses know, and how do you change it?

SOURCE: preview reporting on the rebuilt wanted system (see CNF-010). OBSERVATION: identity vectors (face/clothes/vehicle), hollow-star uncertain states, wardrobe counters. CONTEXT: classic GTA heat was distance + line-of-sight; RDR2 added witness/reporting layers. INFERENCE: GTA VI models knowledge propagation across NPC and police agents — evasion becomes disguise, vehicle-swap, and alibi management.

ANL-004 SEP 2026 HARDWAREOBSERVED

"Captured on base PS5, 4K/30" — what it proves and what it doesn't

The Extended Look's base-PS5 in-engine capture (4K/30, 14.2 GB uncompressed on Rockstar's site) establishes a real running target — but capture conditions, not a shipping guarantee. Preview confirmation of 30 FPS is what locks it.

SOURCE: Rockstar's Extended Look (27 Aug 2026) and preview coverage (CNF-007, CNF-011). OBSERVATION: ~26 min in-engine, base PS5, 4K/30. CONTEXT: marketing capture can use favorable scenes and non-final builds; only hands-on previews test the shipping build. INFERENCE: treat base-PS5 capture as a credible floor for visual ambition — corroborated by the 30 FPS confirm — not as a performance certificate.

ANL-005 OCT 2026 TECHNICALINFERRED

The RAGE technical dossier: streaming-and-budget architecture as the invariant

Across five generations, the load-bearing finding: Rockstar converts each removed hardware constraint into density and content, never into framerate. GTA VI is the clearest case — new SSD and RT headroom, locked 30fps.

SOURCE: full dossier (dossier.html), Sections A–M. OBSERVATION: 17-domain status matrix; EV-01..EV-18 registry; I1–I6 contradictions; UNK-01..12 unknowns. CONTEXT: III's streaming bet (2001) → V's "everything in a streaming state" (2013) → RDR2 evolved RAGE (2018) → Enhanced RT rehearsal (2025) → VI PBR step (2026). INFERENCE: the removed constraint (storage → memory → latency) funds scale; the unremoved CPU constraint sets frame-rate. ASSUMPTION: console targets reflect engine priorities rather than platform quirks — flagged, not proven.
